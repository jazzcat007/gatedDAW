# Contributing to this Fork

Contributions must preserve applicable copyright notices, license terms, and third-party attributions.

## Contribution standard

- Keep pull requests small, focused, and independently reviewable.
- Test the change at a level appropriate to its risk and state what you verified in the pull request.
- The submitting contributor is responsible for the correctness, security, licensing, and maintainability of their change.
- Automation and AI-assisted tools may be used, but they do not replace that responsibility.
- Do not claim to speak for, represent, or be affiliated with the upstream project or its developers unless they have expressly authorized it.

## Attribution and support

- Keep links to upstream accurate when referring to upstream work.
- Send bugs and feature requests for this fork to this repository; do not direct them upstream unless the issue has been reproduced there.
- Do not use upstream names, logos, community credits, funding links, or contact details in a way that implies upstream endorsement.
